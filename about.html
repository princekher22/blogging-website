About this blog

Hi! I am a second year student and DAYLOG is my web design project. Every night I write a short entry about my day: what I learned, how I felt and what I want to do better tomorrow.

The idea is simple. A small daily habit of writing makes busy days feel meaningful.

What I used to build it

  • HTML for the structure of every page
  • CSS for the neon futuristic look
  • JavaScript for the live clock, typing text, mood filter and contact form
  • GitHub Pages to put it online
